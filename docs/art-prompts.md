Generate one single full-body stylized low-poly 3D western gunslinger on a genuinely transparent background with alpha. Broad brim black hat, dark charcoal coat, ochre neckerchief, holstered revolver. Standing three-quarter profile facing right, readable angular silhouette, warm rim light, boots fully in frame with margin. Flat shaded angular geometry, charcoal and ochre palette. Single character only. No floor or scenery, no text, no interface, no watermark. Preserve genuine background transparency.
